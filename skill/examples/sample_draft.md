# AI SEO Tools: 7 Options Worth Paying For in 2026

AI SEO tools promise to cut research and drafting time in half. Most of them do, but each one automates a different slice of the job. This guide compares seven tools we have used on real client sites, with pricing and the one task each does best.

## What an AI SEO tool actually automates

The useful ones cover four jobs: keyword clustering, brief generation, on-page scoring, and internal-link suggestions. A tool that only writes articles is a writing tool, not an SEO tool. Before you pay, check which of the four jobs you are buying.

## How we tested the tools

We ran the same 20 keywords through each tool and scored the briefs against a checklist from [our editorial guide](/editorial-guide). We also checked how the generated titles rendered in Google's result page using the [pixel width rules Google documents](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link).

## The 7 tools compared

- **Surfer** fits teams that already have writers and need on-page scoring.
- **Clearscope** is the strongest for brief quality but costs the most.
- **Frase** is the budget pick for solo creators.
- **MarketMuse** wins on topic authority planning for large sites.
- **NeuronWriter** covers most of Surfer at a lower price.
- **Semrush ContentShake** suits marketers already paying for Semrush.
- **Jasper** is a writing tool with SEO add-ons; buy it for drafting, not research.

## Which AI SEO tools fit a small team

A two-person content team gets the most from one scoring tool plus one clustering tool. Skip all-in-one suites until you publish more than 20 articles a month. The cheapest working stack we found is NeuronWriter plus a free keyword grouper.

## Mistakes to avoid when choosing

Do not pick on article output alone. Check whether the tool explains its scores, whether it exports briefs to your CMS, and whether it supports your language. Half of the tools we tested had weak support for anything other than English.
